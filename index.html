Adventures in Fire Island

The path curved into the park, and the city noise faded behind me. Sunlight slipped through the trees in golden shafts, and the air smelled of cut grass and damp earth. Birds called back and forth overhead as I walked toward a wide meadow dotted with wildflowers. Children chased a kite across the grass while an old man read his newspaper on a bench. At the center of the park, a still pond mirrored the clouds. I sat on a wooden bridge, watched the ducks drift by, and let my thoughts go quiet. By the time the light turned amber, my shoulders had relaxed and I hadn't touched my phone in an hour. I walked out lighter than I came in.
